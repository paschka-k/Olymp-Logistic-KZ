# R5 — Driver, partner and network ideas → v6

Source: `C:\_Projects\Olymp-Logistic-Platform\docs\specs\03_Ideenliste_Nice-to-have_GER.md` (ideas I-11, I-17, I-18, I-24, I-27, I-28 and the open FRs FR-68, FR-70, FR-188, FR-189).
Everything is a mock-up: the KGD register, MDM, SMS / e-mail / WhatsApp / Telegram gateways and partner bids are simulated in the browser; no real API is called and no real credential is used.

## Known limits (mock-up)

- The collection points are a generated sample (names, streets and numbers are fictitious), not the real lab network.
- KGD, MDM and messenger delivery statuses are simulated; the temporary PIN is shown on screen once instead of a real SMS.
- The empty-kilometre cost (240 ₸/km) used for the "empty-run cost covered" share is a plausible default to be verified.
