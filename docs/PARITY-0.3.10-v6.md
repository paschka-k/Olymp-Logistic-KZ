## 3 P1 in detail: 03 Load and weight → Orders → Load & weight

What 0.3.10 shows for trip R-106 (Astana → Kostanay → Aktobe) and v6 does not:

1. **Vehicle type card**: catalogue 3.5 / 6 / 7.5 / 10 / 12.5 t **plus own vehicles** (V01–V04); segments **Thermo zones** (1 zone / 2 zones / no temperature control) and **Body regime** (auto / 2–8 / 15–25); **bulkhead − / +** with the zone 1 | zone 2 split for 2-zone types; facts line (body, pallet places incl. thermo-container places, payload, m³, GVW, empty axle masses, axle limits, internal height, unit, ADR); "Show plan".
2. **Orders table**: height cm, stackable, tag, **third-party cargo T-xxxx** rows (MedSnab Aktobe LLP, pharmacy chain), orders handed to a partner locked; summary boxes pallets / **third-party** / volume / weight / **ADR (Yes · LQ)**.
3. **Loading diagram by chambers**: Chamber 1 · 2–8 °C (thermo containers at the front wall), Chamber 2 · 15–25 °C heated, free slots, **thermo boxes −20 °C (dry ice UN 1845) at the front wall**, LIFO note, side view tallest pallet vs internal height. v6 draws one 2-wide grid with no chambers; on the user's screen the top view overflowed to the right (pallet cells too large, 4th column cut) — fix the SVG scaling (viewBox from the slot count, never wider than the card).
4. **Weight, volume and axle loads**: add "Thermo containers 2–8 °C, pallet places" and "Pallets 15–25 °C" bars, limiting-factor insight line ("volume 38 %, weight 16 % → plan by pallets and m³").
5. **Load checks**: Risk / Watch / Normal levels incl. package check (SSCC), FEFO with LOT and branch, **−20 °C thermo box with dry ice ADR labelling**, **ADR: vehicle approved, driver certificate until**.
6. **Top and side view · centre of gravity**: fact boxes **CoG from the front wall %**, **CoG height cm**, **front axle share %**, **left / right kg (Δ ≤ 1 000)**; ×2 double-stacked marks.
7. **Vehicle match table** for the ticked orders: every catalogue type × zones (1 zone / 2 zones · bulkhead n | m) with pallets per regime, weight, volume, axles, result (recommended / fits / does not fit) and the reason list; "Recommendation — smallest fitting type".
8. **Truck finder**: options "+ demo fleet V05–V07", "+ demo 3.5 t V08, V09", **"spring road limits (01.04–15.05)"**; route legs with km per stop and departure date; **"Restrictions on the travel day"** (e.g. RS-002 bridge over the Ishim ≤ 32 000 kg); rows show pallet places per zone (2–8 / 15–25 / −20 boxes), ADR ✓ / ✗, "roads ✓", other-hub / assigned-to-trip reasons; "How it decides" paragraph.
9. **Realistic demo data**: the load used by the tab must look like R-106: Astana → Kostanay → Aktobe (West), lab / IVD goods (Roche reagents, Sysmex consumables, calibrators, wash solutions ADR LQ, pipette tips, thermo box dry ice), KZ branches, so the screen is informative.
