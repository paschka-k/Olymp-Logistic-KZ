# R3 · Hub inbound — ideas I-1 … I-7 → v6

Source: `Olymp-Logistic-Platform/docs/specs/03_Ideenliste_Nice-to-have_GER.md` (I-1 … I-7, hub / warehouse inbound).
Code: `platform-src/v6/screens/085-hub-inbound.js` (new), small hooks in `080-receiving.js`, help `platform-src/v6/help/hub-inbound.js`,
notes `ibDwell`, `ibSlot`, `ibPre`, `ibDem`, `ibGate` in `screens/003-notes.js`, test `platform-src/tests/e3-hub-inbound.js`.
Mock-up only: state lives in `S.ib` and on the receiving jobs (`S.jobs[].ib`); no backend.

| Idea | What it asks for | v6 place (menu → tab / drawer) | How it works in the mock-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| I-1 Inbound list per hub | Every arriving vehicle with direction, job, priority, status chips; filter per hub | Hubs & warehouses → **Inbound list** | One row per receiving job of all hubs: plate (or "plate to follow"), carrier, direction chip (line haul / pre-carriage / local supplier) with origin → hub, job + linked load + pallets, priority (pharma / ADR high, fixed-slot medium, else low), slot, status chips (receiving status, late, dwell +N, QA hold, temperature, SSCC n/m, plate to follow). Filters hub / direction / priority / status on top of the global hub filter (`hubFilter`). KPI tiles: expected, at gate, dwell overrun, high priority. Row opens the existing job card. |
| I-2 Senders as numbered chips | Unloading points as numbered chips, plain text on hover, colour by status | Inbound list, column "Senders" (also pre-carriage points) | Chip 1…n per sender in unloading order; `title` = "n · name · status". Colour: grey expected, yellow at gate, blue unloading, green received, red problem (QA hold / temperature) or SSCC shortage of that sender's share after a closed reconciliation. |
| I-3 Pre-carriage pickups | Pickup date, forwarder, plate "to follow", kg / m³ / pieces per point; creates an expected inbound | Hubs → **Pre-carriage**, drawer "New pickup" | Form: date, forwarder, plate (empty = to follow), destination hub, zone, up to 3 points with kg / m³ / pieces (validated). Saving creates `PC-nn` and an expected inbound job `IN-Pnn` (not planned, senders = points, places = pieces). Plate can be entered later in the row (reaches the job); "Picked up" needs the plate and marks the job planned. Two seeded pickups. |
| I-4 Ramp time slots | 30-min load / unload slots per gate, fixed appointments, conflicts, booking from the inbound list | Hubs → **Gate slots**, drawer "Gate slot"; "Book slot" button in the Inbound list and on free grid cells | Grid 08:00–20:00 × gates of the chosen hub (respects global hub filter). Slots seeded from job windows; one fixed GDP appointment (IN-0611) and two outbound loading slots (one fixed). Booking checks gate exists, 30-min grid, gate not blocked, gate zone = cargo zone, unloading not finished, no overlap with a fixed appointment; ordinary overlaps are allowed but flagged. Conflict list: overlaps, blocked gate, zone mismatch, with "Remove". Booking sets the job's window, gate and "planned". Audited. |
| I-5 Overdue dwell red with hover | Dwell over limit red, hover with period and minutes, delay yes / no | Inbound list, column "Dwell" | Dwell from gate check-in to unloading end (or hub time); red past `RECV.free`; hover: period hh:mm–hh:mm (running), minutes, free time, overrun, "hub-caused delay: yes / no / —"; delay answer also printed under the value. "+15 min" advances the hub clock. |
| I-6 Demurrage → Costs | Auto-calculated in ₸ from the dwell overrun, booked into Costs as a re-chargeable line | Hubs → **Demurrage**; line appears in Billing → Costs | Amount = ceil(overrun / step) × rate (`RECV`, editable in Settings). States: accruing → ready (after unloading and the delay question) → awaiting 2nd signature → in costs. Four eyes: proposer needs `trips:status`, approver needs `costs:approve` and another role (admin self-approval audited as override). The cost line (`cat: detention`, `ref` = job) carries `recharge` when the delay was not hub-caused: open → invoiced (RC-…) → paid, or waived with a reason ≥ 10 characters. KPI tiles and re-charge table. |
| I-7 Gate check with cold chain | Actual temperature, take over logger data, start SSCC reconciliation | Drawer **Gate check** (button in the job card while the vehicle is at the gate) | 1) actual temperature → `j.chk.temp`; out of range blocks unloading (existing rule) and opens a deviation when a load is linked. 2) logger take-over: 12 readings, min / max, minutes out of range, curve; sets the logger flag. 3) SSCC reconciliation: expected SSCCs from the ops model (`opsPlaces`, read-only) for a linked load, else GS1 SSCCs from the sender's advice (`ssccMake`); scan with or without AI 00, duplicates, foreign and invalid check digits counted; close with a mismatch only with a reason (sets "delay: SSCC mismatch" if unanswered). All steps in the job log and audit. |

## Not done / limits
- The direct "Book to costs" button of the existing receiving card (hub-caused delays) stays as it was; the four-eyes path lives in the Demurrage tab.
- Re-charge status is managed in Hubs → Demurrage; Billing → Costs shows the line (text names the re-charge party) but has no re-charge column (billing is outside R3).
- Logger data and sender SSCC advice are simulated deterministically per job.
